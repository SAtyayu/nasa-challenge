# Reading the Map

The map has two kinds of markers layered on top of each other.

![Global view of city markers colored by environmental risk](images/overview-global.png)

## Marker colors

| Color | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🔴 Red | High Z-Metric city — highest environmental risk |
| 🟠 Orange | Medium-high Z-Metric city |
| 🟡 Yellow | Medium-low Z-Metric city |
| 🟢 Green (small) | Low Z-Metric city — already sustainable |
| 🟢 Green (large) | Optimal settlement location |

The larger green markers are visually distinct from the small green "sustainable city" markers — they represent something different (a candidate location for a *new* settlement, not an existing city). See [Reading a Settlement Card](guide-settlement-card.md) for how to tell them apart at a glance.

## Interacting with markers

- **Click any marker** to open its card — see [Reading a City Card](guide-city-card.md) or [Reading a Settlement Card](guide-settlement-card.md) depending on which kind you clicked.
- **Zoom and pan** as on any Leaflet map — use the `+`/`–` controls in the top-left, or scroll/drag.
- Markers cluster visually in dense regions (e.g. South/Southeast Asia) — zoom in to separate overlapping cities.
